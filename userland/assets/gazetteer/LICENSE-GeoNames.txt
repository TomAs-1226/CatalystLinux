towns.bin is made from GeoNames' gazetteer (https://www.geonames.org/):
cities5000.txt and admin1CodesASCII.txt from
https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/, by tools/gazetteer/build.py,
which keeps each town's name, country, region and position.
