Bezel keeps the foot of its 720 × 720 wall panels free for a dock, even while it is hidden. The handheld has no dock, so that room was wasted. Now lists run to the bottom of the panel and fade out under it; room is kept only where something sits there: a hint line, Home's page dots, buttons, Spotlight's own glass.
